## Context

Built during an internship, **Fireservice** was one of my first professional client deliveries. The brief was a complete website for a security company — design to deployment, handled end-to-end.

## What I Built

- Full custom WordPress theme designed in **Figma**
- **ACF Pro** custom fields for flexible, maintainable content
- Service pages, contact forms, and a clean presentation of the company's offer
- Responsive layout and cross-browser compatibility

## What I Learned

This project was a formative experience in working directly with a client, managing feedback cycles, and delivering production-ready work under a professional deadline.
